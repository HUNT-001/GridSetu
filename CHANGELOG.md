# Changelog

## Unreleased: fast-transfer islanding, recalibrated forecast band, ownership model (November 2026)

### Added
- `python -m gridsetu ownership` and `docs/OWNERSHIP.md`: who owns, runs and pays for one feeder. Recommended model: the DISCOM buys reliability as a service from a local operator (availability fee ₹6.73 lakh a year); smart plugs as a one-off grant (₹16.4 lakh); homes pay nothing extra. The DISCOM gets back a deferred transformer upgrade (₹1.90 lakh a year) and lower losses (₹0.43 lakh a year): net ₹4.40 lakh, ₹8.45 per kWh of outage avoided. Roles, maintenance schedule, training, money flow, open questions. Every input tagged SOURCED or ASSUMED; checked by `tests/test_ownership.py` and `scripts/reproduce.sh`.
- Islanding variant 2: an inverter that is always grid-forming plus a static transfer switch (sense + transfer 4 ms, ABB data sheet). The critical bus is below 0.7 pu for 12 ms and below 0.9 pu for 23 ms, within the ITIC curve; variant 1 (grid-following, 100 ms) is not. `run_islanding` runs all three variants and reports the ITIC check.

### Changed
- Forecast band: cross-conformal calibration over all 8 history weeks (4 folds) instead of the 2 newest, final models fitted on all weeks, and a daily adaptive update from the previous day's misses (adaptive conformal inference). On 6 held-out weeks q10–q90 coverage rose from about 75% to about 80%.

### Results after the full regeneration
- Stress-week forecast coverage 72% → 78% (nominal 80%), median error 4.7% → 4.4%; real-weather backtest 73.6% → 79.2%.
- Monthly evening unserved energy 2,223 → 574 kWh (was 577); home-hours dark 18,167 → 2,483; break-even ₹21.7 per kWh (was ₹21.8). Critical loads, overload trips and feeder outage unchanged.

## Unreleased: Simulink islanding model (November 2026)

### Added
- `matlab/islanding/`: the critical bus through a feeder trip at 0.1 ms steps. Island detection (40 ms), tie opening (60 ms), grid-forming droop control with secondary frequency restoration, a direct-on-line pump start in the island, and synchronised reconnection to a grid that returns 115° out of phase.
- The Simulink model is generated by `build_islanding_model.m` (a MATLAB Function block calling `islanding_step.m`, a Unit Delay for the 16 states, scopes). `run_islanding` runs GridSetu and the baseline, checks Simulink against the plain-loop reference, and writes a summary and a figure. Also runs in Octave without Simulink.

### Found
- The transfer leaves the critical bus at 0.55 pu for 100 ms: fine for lights, fans and the pump, outside ITIC for sensitive electronics. The clinic needs a small UPS, or the inverter should stay grid-forming while connected.

## Unreleased: household model fitted to measured Indian homes (November 2026)

### Changed
- Households now follow Prayas eMARC (`household_model.source: emarc`): the measured average day, a per-home time shift and lognormal size, and random appliance bursts. Fitted to Aug–Oct: 4.9 kWh a day (measured 4.6), evening 0.22 kW (0.21), coincidence 0.30 (0.35).
- The 340 kW target is anchored at the evening peak (`target_peak_window: evening`), so feeder F07 now has 1,090 homes. The first model stays available as `source: legacy` (420 homes).
- Other feeders, the MATLAB export (homes spread over the 21 buses), the business case and the dashboard's house grid follow the home count.
- Fairness Gini is computed over homes that had Tier-3 load to pause (841 of 1,090); the all-homes figure is also reported. 1 test (72).
- Business case costs a smart plug for every home, because every home in the model has some Tier-3 load.

### Results on the fitted homes (all regenerated)
- Monthly evening unserved energy 2,223 → 577 kWh (−74%; was −82%); critical-load outage 0 h; overload trips 13 → 0; home-hours dark 18,167 → 2,484.
- DISCOM calls bounded by the offer: 52 of 60 met, call confidence 90% stated, 89.7% observed. Calls as today: 0 of 60 met in full.
- Fleet, stress week, full adoption: home-hours dark −44% (was −75%). Lifeline frees less power from real homes.
- Business case: ₹9.0 lakh a year (₹69 per home per month); break-even ₹21.8 per kWh.
- Forecast q10–q90 coverage fell to 72% (nominal 80%) on the spikier homes.
